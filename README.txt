Sardar Tours & Travels website
1. Open index.html in a browser (internet needed for Bootstrap, Font Awesome, fonts and map).
2. Upload the whole folder to your hosting (public_html).
3. Gallery: replace the colour .tile blocks with <img src="images/your-photo.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded">.
4. Reviews: replace the placeholder text in index.html with real customer reviews.
5. Social links in the footer use # - add your Facebook/Instagram/YouTube URLs.
